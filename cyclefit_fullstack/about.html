About CycleFit

We guide women through phase-based training that honors energy, recovery, and overall wellness.

1. Menstrual Phase

Focus on gentle movement, stretching, and rest. This phase often calls for lighter cardio and more recovery.

  • Walking
  • Stretching
  • Breathing exercises

2. Follicular Phase

Energy usually rises, so this is a great time for strength training and higher-intensity workouts.

  • Bodyweight circuits
  • Light resistance
  • Yoga flow

3. Ovulation Phase

Many women feel strong and coordinated during this phase, making it ideal for cardio and power-based movement.

  • Interval training
  • Dance cardio
  • Core sessions

4. Luteal Phase

Recovery and balance become important again. Choose moderate routines with extra rest and mindful movement.

  • Pilates
  • Mobility work
  • Low-impact cardio

Why the cycle matters

Training can feel more effective when you adjust intensity to your phase and listen to your body.

Helpful habits

  • Sleep well
  • Hydrate regularly
  • Warm up before every workout
